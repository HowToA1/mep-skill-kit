# Changelog: MEP Skill Kit

*MEP Skill Kit v1.0 · by Dimitar Bidzhekov*

Every change to the kit is recorded here: the version, the date, what changed and why.

## Version 1.0 · 17 November 2026 · first release for the 64th iMEP Porto

**Twelve skills, built for the Delegation of Bulgaria and working on any committee topic**
1. Topic Briefing: a study pack for any committee topic, with quick, standard and deep versions.
2. Bulgaria Position: Bulgaria's position paper, with every claim marked as officially stated, based on laws and votes, or reasoned; plus the head-of-delegation introduction.
3. Resolution: clause ideas before the session; correct MEP formatting and a defence table during committee work.
4. Red Team: a score out of 40, the five biggest weaknesses with fixes, and a predicted speech against; attack mode for other committees.
5. Amendments: the correct format, a defence speech, the committee's 2-minute response, and a fast mode for the General Assembly.
6. Speeches: every MEP speech type, timed, with delivery marks and fill-in gaps for the closing speech.
7. Sparring: eight tough delegate personas, scored answers out of 10 and model answers.
8. Live Copilot: replies of 45 words or fewer, counter-questions and procedural moves within seconds.
9. Fact-Check: a verdict on every claim, the correct version with date and source, and safe wording.
10. Lobbying: interest maps, compromise wording, merged clauses, vote counts and persuasive messages.
11. Session Tracker: a structured record of the whole session, with statistics (tested on sample data).
12. Debrief: evening check-ins, the final report, a handover note, honest posts and feedback on the kit.

**Reference documents included with the skills**
- EU Powers Map: who can do what in the EU, decisions that need unanimity, institutions, and red-flag phrases with safer versions.
- Bulgaria Country Profile, checked 17 November 2026: Prime Minister Rumen Radev (since 8 May 2026), President Iliana Iotova (elected 1 November 2026), the euro since 1 January 2026, Bulgaria's angle by topic, and sensitive issues.
- MEP Procedure and Phrase Bank: resolution format, clause phrases, General Assembly order and timings, voting and etiquette.
- Research Sources: trustworthy sources and rules for quoting.
- Worked Examples: a housing study pack and resolution, to show the standard.

**Changes made during development (same day)**
- The first draft was built only for a housing committee. It was rebuilt to work on any topic once it was confirmed that the delegation represents Bulgaria and the committee topic is not yet known. The housing material was kept only as worked examples.
- Fixed a formatting error in two skill descriptions (Live Copilot and Session Tracker) that would have stopped them from uploading.
- Added the guide page, the plain-language Google Docs and the Google Slides presentation, so delegates never have to read technical files.
- Created the shared Google Drive folder with editor access for the kit's maintainers.

**Quality checks:** all 12 skills pass the official skill validator, every reference file each skill points to is included, and the Session Tracker's statistics were tested on sample data.
