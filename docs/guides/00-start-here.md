## What this kit is

This kit gives Claude (the AI assistant at claude.ai) twelve new abilities, called **skills**, made for Model European Parliament. Once they're installed you can say things like *"We got our topic, help me prepare"* or *"Grill me on our resolution"*, and Claude knows exactly how MEP works, how the EU makes decisions, and how Bulgaria sees the issues.

It works for **any committee topic**: AI, climate, defence, migration, health, enlargement, housing or anything else.

You don't need any technical knowledge. If you can upload a photo, you can install this.

## Install in five minutes (once per person)

1. **Open Claude** at claude.ai on a computer and sign in.
2. **Turn on one setting:** click your name (bottom left) → **Settings** → **Capabilities** → make sure **"Code execution and file creation"** is switched **on**. Skills need it.
3. **Download the install files:** open the **Install files** folder in this Drive and download the 12 .zip files to your computer. Keep them zipped. If you double-click one in Drive you'll see a SKILL.md and a references folder inside; that's normal. That is the skill itself, and Drive simply can't preview it. You can read the same content in the **Skill instructions (readable copies)** folder.
4. **Upload them to Claude:** in Claude go to **Customize** → **Skills** → click the **+** button → **Create skill** → **Upload a skill**, and choose one of the files. Repeat for all 12.
5. **Check:** all 12 skills should appear in your skills list, switched **on**.
6. **In every chat,** make sure **web search** is turned on (in the chat's tools menu) so Claude can check facts live.

**Optional but recommended:** in Claude, create a **Project** called *"iMEP Porto — Bulgaria"* and add your study guide, the session handbook and your position paper to it. Every chat inside that project will already know your context.

## How to use it

You **don't need to remember skill names**. Just ask naturally, and Claude picks the right skill on its own. For example:

- "We got our topic: [paste your committee and question]. Prepare me fast."
- "What is Bulgaria's position on this? Who are our allies?"
- "Here's what our committee agreed. Turn it into a proper resolution."
- "Find every weakness in this resolution before the General Assembly."
- "Quick: the delegate from France says our plan has no legal basis. What do I answer?"

If you want a specific skill, you can name it: *"Use the red-team skill on this."*

## Which model to choose

At the top of every chat in Claude you can choose a model. Use this simple rule:

| Model | Best for | Think of it as |
|---|---|---|
| **Opus 5.5** | Research, drafting resolutions, strategy, fact-checking | The careful expert |
| **Sonnet 5.5** | Speeches, practice sessions, amendments, everyday use | The fast, excellent all-rounder |
| **Haiku 5.5** | Quick answers on your phone during the session | The sprinter |
| **Fable 5.2** | The hardest check of your final resolution, if your plan includes it | The top expert |

**Tip:** for drafting and red-teaming, also switch on **extended thinking** in the chat. Claude takes a little longer but reasons more carefully.

## Rules that make you look better, not worse

- **The session's rules always win.** If the handbook says you can't bring a pre-written resolution, the kit helps you collect *ideas* instead of a finished text.
- **Understand everything you say.** The skills explain their reasoning. Read it, so the words are truly yours.
- **Every number needs a date and a source.** Run the fact-check before you speak.
- **Respect device rules.** If phones aren't allowed in the plenary, use the kit during breaks.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't log or post other students' names or photos without their permission.
- **Sensitive Bulgarian topics** (Russia and Ukraine, North Macedonia, the euro): use official government wording only. Document 02 explains how.

## If something doesn't work

- **A skill won't upload:** make sure you uploaded the .zip exactly as downloaded (not unzipped) and that "Code execution and file creation" is on.
- **"I opened a zip and can't read SKILL.md":** that's expected in Drive's preview. Read the matching Google Doc in the **Skill instructions (readable copies)** folder instead.
- **Claude doesn't seem to use the skill:** name it in your request ("use the speeches skill").
- **Claude says it can't search the web:** turn on web search in the chat's tools menu.
- **Anything else:** write to Dimitar.
