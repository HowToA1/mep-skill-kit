# Research Sources for Any MEP Topic

*MEP Skill Kit v1.0 · by Dimitar Bidzhekov*

Ranked by trust. Start at the top; use the lower tiers for reactions and colour, not for the core facts.

## Tier 1: primary EU sources
| Need | Where | Search tip |
|---|---|---|
| What laws exist or are proposed | **EUR-Lex** (eur-lex.europa.eu) | Search "[topic] regulation" or "[topic] directive"; check "in force" status |
| Where a law is in the process | **Legislative Observatory, OEIL** (oeil.secure.europarl.europa.eu) | Procedure numbers look like 2026/0268(COD) |
| A clear 8–12 page explainer | **EPRS / EP Think Tank** (europarl.europa.eu/thinktank) | "EPRS briefing [topic]". This is the single best starting point |
| What Parliament said | europarl.europa.eu/news (press), "Texts adopted" | "European Parliament resolution [topic] 2026" |
| What the Commission plans | commission.europa.eu; the Commission work programme; "Have Your Say" | "[topic] strategy", "action plan", "Commission proposal" |
| What member states agreed | consilium.europa.eu (conclusions, press releases) | "Council conclusions [topic]" |
| What leaders want | European Council conclusions | The most recent summit |
| Data | **Eurostat** (ec.europa.eu/eurostat); Eurobarometer (citizens' opinions) | "Eurostat [indicator] [year]"; note dataset codes |
| Courts | curia.europa.eu | Case numbers like C-724/18 |
| Agencies | EEA (environment), ECDC/EMA (health), Frontex/EUAA (migration), ENISA (cyber), Europol, FRA (rights), Eurofound (social) | The agency's annual report |

## Tier 2: strong secondary sources
- OECD, IMF, World Bank, UN agencies (international comparisons)
- Think tanks across the spectrum: Bruegel, CEPS, ECFR, EPC, Jacques Delors Institute,
  Robert Schuman Foundation, Wilfried Martens Centre (centre-right), FEPS (centre-left),
  Green European Foundation, New Direction (conservative). Use several to stay balanced.
- Quality EU press: Euractiv, Politico Europe, Euronews, Eunews, Agence Europe, Reuters, AP.

## Tier 3: Bulgaria
- BTA (bta.bg/en), BNR English, gov.bg, mfa.bg, president.bg, parliament.bg, NSI (nsi.bg), Bulgarian National Bank (bnb.bg).
- Capital.bg and Mediapool for analysis. Novinite for English summaries (check against BTA).

## Rules for quoting
1. Every number gets a **date** and a **source** ("Eurostat, 2025 data").
2. Prefer the **latest** release; say "provisional" if it is.
3. Don't mix EU-27 and euro-area figures, or nominal and real values, without saying so.
4. If two sources disagree, quote the primary one or say "around X".
5. Quote statements from politicians only with the date and the outlet.
6. Never invent a statistic, a quote, a study or a law. If it can't be found, say so.
