# Bulgaria Delegation Position Builder

*MEP Skill Kit v1.0 · by Dimitar Bidzhekov*

A delegation with a clear position has three advantages: it knows what to fight for, it
doesn't sign everything, and it can build coalitions on purpose. The risk is putting words
in the Bulgarian government's mouth, so be scrupulously honest about what is documented.

## Inputs
- **Topic or committee question** (required; ask if missing).
- Optional: which delegate sits on which committee (to tailor allies and questions), and
  the delegate's personal angle.

## Workflow
1. **Start from `references/bulgaria-profile.md`.** It covers politics as of 17 Nov 2026,
   EU milestones, numbers, topic angles and sensitive issues.
2. **Search for the current official position** (see section 7 of the profile): statements
   by the Council of Ministers, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the President; how
   Bulgaria voted in the Council; how Bulgarian MEPs voted (HowTheyVote.eu). Search in both
   English and Bulgarian (for example "позиция на България [тема]").
3. **Label every stance claim:**
   - **Stated:** official statement (cite with date).
   - **Policy record:** inferred from laws or votes (cite them).
   - **Inferred:** reasoned from Bulgaria's interests. Say so plainly.
4. **Check feasibility** against `references/eu-powers-map.md`, so Bulgaria's asks are
   legally realistic.
5. **Map the room:** group the other delegations into likely clusters for this topic (for
   example frontline and border states, net contributors, cohesion countries, neutral
   states, big founding members, Nordics and Baltics, the Visegrád group, the Mediterranean).
   Mark which are evidence-based and which are hypotheses.
6. **Write the paper**, then the head-of-delegation intro if asked.

## Output format
```
# Position Paper — Delegation of Bulgaria
Committee: ... | Question: ... | 64th iMEP Porto, 19–26 Nov 2026

## Bulgaria in numbers on this topic (4–6 dated stats)
## What Bulgaria has done (laws, programmes, votes — labelled)
## Our position on the main options
| Option / EU measure | Support / Conditional / Oppose | Why (Bulgarian interest) | Label |
## Our three asks
## Red lines
## Allies, opponents, swing delegations
| Delegation(s) | Likely stance | Evidence or hypothesis | How to win them |
## Hard questions Bulgaria will face (5), with 30-second answers
   — include at least one on Bulgaria's own weak spot
## One-sentence position: "The delegation of Bulgaria believes..."
## Sources
```

## Head-of-delegation introduction (if asked)
Check the session's time limit; handbooks vary (1.5–3 minutes). Structure: a greeting to
the President and Porto (a single "Bom dia, Porto" works); who we are (one memorable Bulgarian
fact); what we care about across all committees (3 themes); a promise of constructive
cooperation; a closing line. About 120–130 spoken words per minute.

## Quality bar
- Never fabricate a minister's quote, a vote or a government position.
- Treat sensitive issues (Russia and Ukraine, North Macedonia, the euro referendum call,
  corruption) with care: official wording, dates, no outlet's labels. See the profile, section 5.
- Bulgaria's weak spots will be raised by others, so prepare honest answers in advance.
- If the session lets delegates speak from personal conviction rather than as government
  envoys, offer a "Bulgarian youth perspective" variant alongside the official one.
