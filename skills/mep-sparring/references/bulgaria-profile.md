## 1. Political snapshot (VERIFIED)
- **Prime Minister: Rumen Radev** (Progressive Bulgaria). He resigned as President in
  January 2026 to run in parliamentary elections and was elected Prime Minister by
  Parliament on **8 May 2026** (124 votes to 70, 36 abstentions), after Progressive Bulgaria
  won the **19 April 2026** election, the eighth in five years, with an outright majority.
  [BTA; Bernama; Al Jazeera]
- **President: Iliana Iotova.** Acting president from January 2026; won the presidential
  runoff on **1 November 2026** with about 57.6% against Andrey Gyurov, becoming Bulgaria's
  first elected female head of state. Turnout was record-low. [Reuters via The Star; Brussels
  Signal]
- Key ministers (May 2026): Deputy PM and Finance Galab Donev; Deputy PM and Economy
  Alexander Pulev; Foreign Affairs Velislava Petrova-Chamova; Defence Dimitar Stoyanov;
  Interior Ivan Demerdzhiev. [BTA] Check for reshuffles.
- How commentators describe the government varies sharply: "centre-left" (AP), "sovereignty-
  oriented", "Russia-friendly" (Reuters and others). Radev called for a referendum on the
  euro before adoption, which Parliament rejected. **Do not adopt any outlet's label.** Base
  the delegation's stance on official government statements and votes.
- The new government's stated near-term tasks included the budget, inflation, and judicial
  and anti-corruption reform linked to unlocking EU funds. [allmind/Reuters summary]

## 3. Economy and society in numbers
- House prices: **+15.5%** year on year in Q2 2026, second-fastest in the EU (Eurostat via BTA). VERIFIED
- Mortgage rates around 2.5–3.5%; construction costs up 15–18% in 2025 (industry data). VERIFIED (reported)
- Young people leave the parental home at an average age of **28.2** (2024, Eurostat). VERIFIED
- GDP per capita in purchasing power is the **lowest in the EU** *(verify the latest Eurostat figure)*.
- Population about 6.4 million and shrinking, one of the fastest population declines in the
  world through emigration and low birth rates *(verify)*. **Demography** is a Bulgarian
  angle on almost any topic.
- Energy: the Kozloduy nuclear plant supplies roughly a third of electricity; plans exist for
  new units; coal regions (Maritsa East, Stara Zagora) face the just-transition question *(verify the figures)*.
- Geography: Black Sea coast and the EU's external border with Türkiye; the Danube border
  with Romania; neighbour to North Macedonia and Serbia (enlargement).
- Black Sea security is live: in 2026 the PM reported a drone attack that sank a ship off
  Bulgaria's coast (Al Jazeera headline). *(verify the details before citing)*

## 5. Sensitive issues: handle with care
- **Russia, Ukraine and NATO:** positions differ between the current government, the
  President, opposition parties and public opinion. Quote **official statements** with dates.
  In MEP, a delegate can say "The delegation of Bulgaria, while supporting [X], stresses [Y]".
- **North Macedonia:** emotionally charged. Stick to the official conditions (constitutional
  amendments, history commission) and use respectful language.
- **The euro:** adopted, but the PM had called for a referendum. Frame it as "Bulgaria's first
  year in the euro: what we've learnt".
- **Corruption and rule of law:** acknowledge the problem honestly; Bulgaria gains
  credibility by naming its own challenges before others do.
- In MEP the students are not diplomats bound by instructions. Many sessions let delegates
  argue from their country's perspective **and** their own values. Check the session norms.
