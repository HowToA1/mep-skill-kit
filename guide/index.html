Install in five minutes

You'll receive 12 .zip files (one per skill) plus a full bundle. Upload the 12 single zips; don't unzip them.

  1. In Claude open Settings → Capabilities and switch on Code execution and file creation. Skills need it.
  2. Go to Customize → Skills, click +, then Create skill → Upload a skill.
  3. Upload each zip, one at a time, until all 12 appear in your list, switched on.
  4. Turn on web search in your chat. Most skills check facts live.
  5. Create a Project called “iMEP Porto — Bulgaria” and add the study guide, the session handbook and your position paper. Every chat in it starts with your context.

You never have to name a skill. Just ask naturally and Claude picks the right one. Naming one (“use mep-red-team on this”) also works.

Rules that make you look better, not worse

  • The session's rules win. If pre-written resolutions aren't allowed, the drafter builds a clause bank instead.
  • Understand every word you say. Each skill explains its reasoning. Read it.
  • Every number gets a date and a source. Run the fact-checker before you speak.
  • Respect device rules in plenary. If phones aren't allowed, use the copilot in breaks.
  • Privacy: don't log or post other students' names or photos without their permission.
  • Bulgaria's sensitive issues (Russia and Ukraine, North Macedonia, the euro) are handled with official wording only.
